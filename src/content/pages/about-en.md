I'm from Malaysia. I studied geology at the University of Malaya, and I now do earthquake research at Kyoto University in Japan.

The name "金釘子" means Golden Spike. In geology, a Golden Spike (GSSP, Global Boundary Stratotype Section and Point) is the reference point that defines a boundary in the geologic time scale. I hope this channel can be a starting point for getting to know the Earth.

## What you'll find here

- **Videos**: one geology or earthquake idea per episode, in about five minutes (in Chinese).
- **Blog**: written versions of the videos, plus life and travel in Japan.
- **Quake monitor**: a live map of earthquakes around the world.
- **Quizzes and specimens**: test yourself, and browse rocks I've photographed.
